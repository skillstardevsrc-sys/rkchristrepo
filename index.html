Frequently Asked Questions

What types of sarees and jewellery do you offer?

−

We offer a curated range of premium sarees including silk, cotton, and designer collections, along with timeless jewellery such as necklaces, earrings, bangles and sets crafted for every occasion.

How does the Custom Blouse Stitching service work?

+

Simply choose your preferred neckline and sleeve design, submit your measurements online or via WhatsApp, and our master tailors will handcraft your blouse to a flawless bespoke fit.

How do I provide my measurements for blouse stitching?

+

You can enter your measurements in our simple online form or connect with our sizing specialists directly over WhatsApp for step-by-step guidance.

Do you offer worldwide delivery and what is the timeline?

+

Yes, we ship globally! Domestic orders in India are delivered within 4–7 business days, while international shipments typically arrive in 7–12 business days.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept all major Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, Net Banking, and secure international payment gateways via Razorpay with 100% bank-grade encryption.

How can I reach customer support or track my order?

+

You can track your order status directly via the tracking link sent to your email and WhatsApp, or message our dedicated support team anytime at +91 99943 04884.
